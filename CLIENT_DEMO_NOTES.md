# Client Demo Notes

## Recommended opening

“This is not a static mock-up. It is a working prototype designed to validate how a consolidated portfolio-management experience could operate across both public and private investments. The data is illustrative; the purpose today is to validate workflows, reporting, controls and integration requirements before production implementation.”

## What to emphasize

- One view across liquid and illiquid investments.
- Public/private/consolidated scopes.
- Multi-currency reporting.
- Private-market metrics and portfolio-company look-through.
- Compliance rules evaluated against the book.
- Reconciliation as a first-class operational workflow-not an afterthought.
- Board/client reporting.
- Clear separation between the presentation layer and production data connectors.

## What not to claim

Do not say the prototype currently:

- connects to real custodians or fund administrators;
- uses live exchange prices or licensed benchmark feeds;
- performs production-grade investment accounting;
- has enterprise authentication/RBAC;
- provides immutable audit logs;
- has completed security, regulatory or penetration testing;
- is suitable for live trading or booking transactions.

## Useful closing question for the client

“Which part of this operating model is most important to you: consolidated visibility, reporting, private-market monitoring, compliance, reconciliation, or transaction/operations workflow? That will tell us which integrations and modules should be prioritized in the production scope.”
